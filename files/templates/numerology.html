Calculation rules

Letters use the Pythagorean cycle: A=1 through I=9, then J=1 through R=9, and S=1 through Z=8. Spaces and punctuation are ignored.

  • Life Path: reduce all birth-date digits.
  • Personal Year: reduce birth month + birth day + current calendar year.
  • Expression: reduce all name letters.
  • Soul Urge: reduce vowels A, E, I, O, U.
  • Personality: reduce consonants. Y is treated as a consonant.
  • Master numbers: 11, 22, and 33 are preserved.

Calculated locally in your browser. Numerology is an interpretive practice, not a scientific assessment or prediction.
